Inject CoCo into the Twenty tab

This page generates a one-click bookmarklet that loads coco-sdk.js and inject.js into the currently open Twenty tab. The gateway must be reachable from your browser at the URL you configured — default is http://localhost:8080.

Step 1 — drag this to your bookmarks bar

CoCo → Twenty

Right-click the link → "Add to bookmarks" if your browser blocks drag-to-bar.

Step 2 — open a Twenty tab (localhost:3000 by default)

After Twenty loads, click the CoCo → Twenty bookmark.

A floating CoCo badge appears in the bottom-right corner. Toasts render on every verdict.

Step 3 — drive a scenario (optional)

Watch verdicts fire by running:

python -m agents.cli --scenario deal_stage_move_allow

The agent calls the gateway; the Twenty tab sees each verdict through the SDK's onVerdict callback.

Alternative — paste into DevTools

If bookmarklets are blocked, paste this into the Twenty tab's DevTools console:

var s=document.createElement('script');s.src='http://localhost:8080/sdk/coco-sdk.js';s.onload=function(){var i=document.createElement('script');i.src='http://localhost:8080/sdk/inject.js';document.head.appendChild(i);};document.head.appendChild(s);
